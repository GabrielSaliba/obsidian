### <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Order State Model</span>

Payment webhooks own payment transitions; admins own production and shipping commands; delivery callbacks or jobs own delivery; cancellation and refund use dedicated commands.

- Admins não podem alterar status providenciados de APIs ou serviços externos para não causar inconsistência. 
- Verificar se clientes terão poder de cancelar pedidos ou somente será possível comunicando a loja.


### <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Disponibilty and Concurrency Rules</span>
 Direct checkout is restricted to replenishable or made-to-order products; unique, scarce, customizable, or option-dependent products use <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">WhatsApp</mark>. This restriction must be enforced by admin validation and documented as a business invariant.
