# Game Design Document

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)"> 1.</span> <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Game Overview</span>

**Genre:**

- Management Simulator
- Cozy Simulation

**Core Inspiration:**

- Shop management inspired by Supermarket Simulator
- Calm store atmosphere inspired by inKombini

**Game Concept:**  
The player manages their own **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">bakery / café / convenience-style store</mark>**, taking care of stocking shelves, organizing products, preparing drinks, serving customers, and running the register.

The experience is focused on a **<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">cozy and satisfying daily routine</mark>**, where the player slowly improves the store, unlocks new products, and builds a comfortable place for customers.

The gameplay is **relaxing, immersive, and management-focused**, with progression centered around expanding the business and keeping the shop organized.

---

## <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">2.</span> <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Core Gameplay Loop</span>

1. **Open the store**
2. **Buy and receive products**
3. **Store items in stockroom, shelves, and fridges**
4. **Prepare café items and bakery products**
5. **Attend customers and complete sales**
6. **Close the day and review profits**
7. **Upgrade and expand the store**

The core loop is based on:

> **Buy → Organize → Serve → Sell → Improve**

---

## <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">3</span><span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">. Core Mechanics</span>

### 3.1 Stock Management

- Buy products from suppliers
- Receive and store boxes
- Organize items in shelves, stockroom, and fridges
- Refill products when needed<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)"></span>

### 3.2 Shelf Organization

- Place products manually
- Organize categories and displays
- Keep the store visually tidy and functional

### 3.3 Café Service

- Prepare coffee and simple food items
- Serve customer orders
- Bring items to tables

### 3.4 Checkout

- Register products
- Accept payment
- Verify and give correct change

### 3.5 Business Management

- Manage profits
- Expand product variety
- Upgrade equipment and store space
- Improve the overall customer experience

## 4. Theme & Atmosphere

The game aims for a **cozy, calm, and satisfying atmosphere**.

### **Tone**

- Peaceful daily routine
- Small neighborhood business feeling
- Warm and welcoming environment

### **Visual Direction**

- Soft cozy colors
- Cute bakery / café presentation
- Clean and satisfying organization
- Small convenience-store inspired layout

### **Audio Direction**

- Calm ambient music
- Soft store sound design
- Relaxing day-to-day mood

---

## 5. Progression

The player gradually grows the business by unlocking:

- New products
- More shelves and fridges
- Better coffee machines
- More customer seating
- Store decoration and layout improvements

The long-term fantasy is:

> **Turning a small simple shop into a complete and charming bakery/café.**

---

## 6. Main Appeal

What makes the game appealing is the satisfaction of:

- Organizing products neatly
- Managing your own store
- Serving customers
- Watching the shop slowly improve
- Building a cozy place with your own style

The game should feel rewarding even during simple tasks.

---

## 7. Scope Direction

The project should start small and focused.

### **Core First Version**

- Small bakery/café store
- Shelf and fridge stocking
- Product purchasing
- Basic café preparation
- Checkout system
- Daily income and progression
