# <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Game Design Document</span>

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">1. Game Overview</span>

**Genre:**

- Action Adventure
- Third Person Shooter

---

**Core Inspiration:**

- Narrative tone and structure inspired by Violet Evergarden
- Fast combat with hordes
- Stylized European steampunk world

---

**Game Concept:**  
The player is a **combat courier** traveling between **European-inspired cities** delivering important letters.

Each mission consists of:

- Traveling through dangerous routes
- Fighting <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">enemy hordes and machines</span>
- Using <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">mobility + rifle mechanics</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Delivering the letter at the destination</span>

The gameplay is **<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">fast, fluid, and aggressive</mark>**, mixing **acrobatic movement and shooting** with **emotional story delivery moments**.
