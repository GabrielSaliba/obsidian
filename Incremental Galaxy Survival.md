# <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Game Design Document<br></span>
## <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">1. Game Overview</span>

**Genre:**
- Survival Shooter
- Incremental / Roguelite

**Core Inspiration:**
- Planet hopping similar to <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Super Mario Galaxy</mark>
- Bullet-hell survival gameplay inspired by <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Vampire Survivors</mark>

**Game Concept:**  
The player pilots a small spaceship exploring **tiny planets across a stylized galaxy**. Each planet is overrun by <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">alien swarms</span>. Survive <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">waves of enemies, collect resources, and upgrade your ship</span> in real time while orbiting planets.
<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Defeat the planet boss and fast travel to the next more dangerous planet</span>

The gameplay is **<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">fast, chaotic, and addictive</mark>**, with an **incremental progression system** that allows permanent upgrades between runs.
