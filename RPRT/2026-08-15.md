## <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Decisions</span>

- A provider call remains counted after a post-reservation crash or unknown response because the system cannot prove that the request did not reach Resend.
- A safe Resend retry uses the same job, business key, idempotency key, and request fingerprint inside the original 24-hour window.
- Replay keeps the same job to preserve counters, policy limits, and audit history. It keeps the same business key to prevent the provider from treating the replay as a new external effect.
- A changed fingerprint, exhausted call budget, or expired idempotency window moves the job to `dead_letter` instead of permitting an automatic call.
- Technical-lead approval makes the RPRT-43 contract normative for RPRT-52 implementation.
