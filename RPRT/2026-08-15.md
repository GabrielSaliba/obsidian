### **Operation Matrix**

- **Automatic call:** The worker makes a provider call under the job’s normal retry policy. No administrator action is necessary. It stops when `automatic_call_limit` is reached.
- **Replay:** An administrator authorizes one additional call after the job is dead-lettered. It requires recorded safety proof and is allowed only for replayable job types.

A replay does **not** reset the automatic call count. Example: an email job allows 5 automatic calls plus 1 replay, for a maximum lifetime total of 6 provider calls.


- **Total timeout:** Maximum time allowed for the complete call, from its start until the full response arrives. It usually includes the connection time.

Example: connection timeout = 5 seconds, total timeout = 30 seconds. The call must connect within 5 seconds and finish within 30 seconds.


- **Backoff base:** The initial delay used to calculate wait times between retries. For exponential backoff, a 2-second base can produce delays such as 2, 4, 8, and 16 seconds.
- **Backoff cap:** The maximum permitted delay between retries. If the cap is 10 seconds, the sequence becomes 2, 4, 8, 10, and 10 seconds.

Jitter can add randomness to these delays to prevent many jobs from retrying at the same time.
