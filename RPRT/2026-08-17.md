## <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Decisions</span>

- UptimeRobot will monitor only production from M7. Protected staging does not need a persistent external monitor or a protection bypass.
- Safe health endpoints can exist in all environments for authorized deployment, CI, and smoke checks. They must be bounded, read-only, low cost, and free of secret or provider details.
- RPRT-61 remains completed M1 account preparation. RPRT-63 owns final URLs, backup push verification, monitor activation, and controlled alert evidence in M7.
- Administrative recovery-case closure is an emergency resolution, not payment truth. It cannot assign settlement, authorize fulfillment, create a refund, or create payment-approved email work.
- `APP_ENVIRONMENT` is a declared application identity. The app verifies it against branch, Vercel, repository, Supabase project, and secret-scope evidence instead of trusting `NODE_ENV` or `VERCEL_ENV` alone.
- Feature Preview isolation IDs are generated server-side from project, repository, and case-sensitive branch data. They are test-data namespaces, not authorization tokens, and no business feature consumes them yet.

## <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Issues</span>

- Moving the 3-point monitoring activation issue to M7 made the previous M1 estimate and forecast stale. Both values are now corrected in the plan and control issue.
- A missing `APP_ENVIRONMENT` does not prevent the `next dev` process from printing `Ready`; validation runs when the root layout is evaluated. A real page request returned HTTP 500, and `pnpm build` failed, as required. The local configuration also lacks `SUPABASE_PROJECT_REF`.
