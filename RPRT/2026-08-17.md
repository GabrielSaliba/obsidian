## <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Decisions</span>

- UptimeRobot will monitor only production from M7. Protected staging does not need a persistent external monitor or a protection bypass.
- Safe health endpoints can exist in all environments for authorized deployment, CI, and smoke checks. They must be bounded, read-only, low cost, and free of secret or provider details.
- RPRT-61 remains completed M1 account preparation. RPRT-63 owns final URLs, backup push verification, monitor activation, and controlled alert evidence in M7.
- Administrative recovery-case closure is an emergency resolution, not payment truth. It cannot assign settlement, authorize fulfillment, create a refund, or create payment-approved email work.
- `APP_ENVIRONMENT` is a declared application identity. The app verifies it against branch, Vercel, repository, Supabase project, and secret-scope evidence instead of trusting `NODE_ENV` or `VERCEL_ENV` alone.
- Feature Preview isolation IDs are generated server-side from project, repository, and case-sensitive branch data. They are test-data namespaces, not authorization tokens, and no business feature consumes them yet.
- Paid cancellation uses one immutable `full_refund_then_cancel` intent. The intent, exact refund-request transition, audit evidence, and refund job commit together and cannot later change or be withdrawn.
- Active intent blocks production, readiness, label creation, carrier handoff, pickup, and local handoff. The first command to commit under the order lock decides the result.
- Intent can stop only an unclaimed label job. A processing, ambiguous, completed, or provider-confirmed label fails cancellation closed until RPRT-25 proves a safe provider cancellation path.
- Paid pre-handoff cancellation keeps reservations `consumed` and restores all direct-stock quantity through one immutable restoration fact per reservation, or restores none.
- Verified refund after handoff preserves fulfillment, stock, and settlement history, opens separate operational recovery, and creates no new customer email work.
- Verified provider checkout cancellation maps the exact payment attempt to `failed` with reason `provider_checkout_cancelled_verified`. No `cancelled` payment-attempt state is added.

## <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Issues</span>

- UptimeRobot Free could not monitor protected Vercel staging without weakening protection or putting a bypass secret in a URL. Production-only monitoring removes this conflict without changing staging security.
- Moving the 3-point monitoring activation issue to M7 made the previous M1 estimate and forecast stale. Both values are now corrected in the plan and control issue.
- A missing `APP_ENVIRONMENT` does not prevent the `next dev` process from printing `Ready`; validation runs when the root layout is evaluated. A real page request returned HTTP 500, and `pnpm build` failed, as required. The local configuration also lacks `SUPABASE_PROJECT_REF`.
- The previous architecture allowed generic refund behavior and did not apply cancellation intent to every forward fulfillment command. The RPRT-42 contract now binds intent to the accepted settlement and exact refund-request transition and applies one shared guard.
- A shipping-label worker could otherwise create an external label after intent commits. The contract now rechecks intent under the order lock before dispatch and fails closed when label truth is already processing, ambiguous, complete, or provider-confirmed.
- Unpaid cancellation previously had no canonical verified provider-cancellation result. The contract maps verified provider cancellation to `failed` and requires all attempts to be terminal non-payable before stock can release.
