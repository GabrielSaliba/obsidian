### Product Status Contract

| Status | Public behavior | Operational meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `draft` | Hidden from public product, media, and placement reads | Editable and unpublished |
| `published` | Visible when the category path is active and package rules pass | Available catalog product |
| `unavailable` | Still visible when public visibility rules pass | Kept in the catalog but not currently available |

> [!warning] No Soft Delete
> RPRT-50 has no `archived` status and no `deleted_at` field. Application roles have no product `DELETE` grant or delete policy. Moving a product to `draft` unpublishes it but does not delete it.

## <span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Decisions</span>

- Keep product status limited to `draft`, `published`, and `unavailable`.
- Use `draft` to unpublish a product. Do not describe this operation as deletion.
- Keep `unavailable` publicly visible when category visibility rules pass.
- Do not add `archived`, `deleted_at`, or application delete access without a separate retention and recovery decision.
- Serialize publication and inherited-package removal with one category advisory guard and no cross-table row-lock inversion.
- Keep posts, newsletter capture, Storage policies, category seed data, generated types, upload helpers, caching, and full-text search outside RPRT-50.
