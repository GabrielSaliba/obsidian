Read the lines correctly

Important structural caveats

  1. webhook_event_subjects.subject_id is polymorphic. Dashed edges are command-validated, not database foreign keys.
  2. domain_transitions.aggregate_id is polymorphic and is not drawn as a normal FK.
  3. Orders and payment attempts form a deliberate cycle: attempts belong to an order; the order keeps optional current and settlement pointers.
  4. Composite foreign keys enforce same-order and same-product identity. A single-column drawing would hide an important safety rule.
  5. Partial unique indexes enforce one active payable attempt, one non-revoked guest capability, and one active or consumed reservation per item.
  6. Recovery, cancellation, subject, and payment-fact records are append-only through triggers. Cardinality alone does not show this protection.
  7. A shipping quote is consumed once by command logic, but orders.shipping_quote_id is not structurally unique.
  8. Staff columns reference auth.users. Guarded triggers and commands prove administrator membership when evidence is inserted.
  9. private.payment_evidence_facts is not part of generated public application types.
  10. The PR implements the database authority boundary, not provider adapters, webhook signature routes, browser UI, guest JWT minting, or scheduler runtime.
