Cancel safely after payment.

A visual map of how immutable cancellation intent, verified refund evidence, order-lock races, consumed-stock restoration, and post-handoff recovery work together.

The central rule: a paid order is not cancelled when staff asks. The system first stores immutable intent and requests a full refund. Only verified refund evidence can cancel the order, stop fulfillment, and return stock.

The mental model

Remote refund work cannot be inside a PostgreSQL transaction. The contract splits the operation into durable intent, an external call, and verified local convergence.

Two atomic boundaries. The first transaction creates durable intent and refund work. The second applies verified refund truth and every local cancellation effect. A remote API call never sits inside either database transaction.

Intent is not cancellation

The order remains placed. Intent only freezes forward work and starts refund processing.

Refund request is not proof

Timeout, dead letter, lost response, elapsed time, and staff statements cannot change payment to refunded.

History is not authority

The settlement pointer remains historical evidence after refund. Current state and command guards decide authority.

Paid cancellation, step by step

Refund request

payment · paid → refund_pending

refund_request_transition_id proves that staff accepted this exact settlement attempt for full-refund cancellation.

Verified refund

payment · refund_pending → refunded

refund_transition_id proves that authenticated provider evidence confirmed the exact full refund.

Order cancellation

order · placed → cancelled

cancellation_transition_id proves that the order and fulfillment cancelled in the same transaction as verified refund convergence.

Why transitions 02 and 03 are both stored on each restoration: refund proof alone cannot return stock, and an unrelated cancellation cannot return paid stock. The pair proves verified refund and matching order cancellation happened together.
What request_paid_order_cancellation must do
  1. Authenticate authorized staff and require a safe non-blank reason plus idempotency key.
  2. Lock the order first and require placed.
  3. Require fulfillment to be unfulfilled, in_production, or ready_for_fulfillment, with no handoff.
  4. Reject processing, ambiguous, completed, or provider-confirmed label evidence until RPRT-25 proves safe label cancellation.
  5. Lock the exact settlement attempt and require it to be paid.
  6. Insert intent, transition payment to refund_pending, write audit, and create one refund job.
  7. Do not yet cancel, restore stock, clear settlement, or send customer email.

The shipping-label sub-race

Intent may win

Unclaimed pending job

The job does not block intent. The worker must lock the order, recheck, record a permanent no-call result, and never dispatch.

Cancellation rejects

Processing job

A provider call can already be in flight. Cancellation fails closed until provider cancellation is proven safe.

Cancellation rejects

Ambiguous result

The provider might have created a label. Local absence is not proof.

Cancellation rejects

Completed or confirmed

Known label truth wins. RPRT-25 must later define a safe provider label-cancellation path.

05

Stock returns without rewriting history

What changes

  • One immutable restoration row is inserted for every eligible consumed reservation.
  • products.stock_quantity increases by each immutable reservation quantity.
  • The complete direct-stock set restores together, including from in_production.
  • Assisted orders have no reservation or stock effect.

What never changes

  • A consumed reservation never moves to released.
  • Partial restoration is forbidden.
  • Duplicate refund evidence cannot increment quantity twice.
  • Post-handoff refund never restores stock automatically.
Select

Every reservation consumed by the exact accepted settlement, and no unrelated reservation.

06

Unpaid and post-handoff branches

Unpaid cancellation

Prove every attempt cannot become paid

  • not_started can be superseded only with proof that no provider call occurred and an audit reason.
  • creating, creation_unknown, and pending block cancellation until verified evidence resolves them.
  • Verified provider cancellation maps the exact attempt to failed with reason provider_checkout_cancelled_verified.
  • Verified provider expiry maps to expired. No payment cancelled state is added.
  • Only then can active reservations release exactly once.
Post-handoff refund

Record conflict; do not rewrite delivery truth

  • Record the accepted settlement attempt as refunded.
  • Keep order, fulfillment, consumed reservations, stock, and settlement pointer unchanged.
  • Continue post-handoff tracking from authoritative handoff evidence.
  • Open one separate recovery case and emit an independent operational alert.
  • Administrative resolution records the operational outcome only.
Late payment never reopens a cancelled order. Verified late paid truth enters the cancelled-order refund recovery path. Duplicate-payment refund also cannot satisfy the accepted-settlement cancellation intent or restore its stock.

Authorization

Customers, guests, normal staff access, workers, anon, and authenticated cannot directly mutate evidence tables. Narrow guarded commands derive actor identity and use fixed safe search_path.

Idempotency

One intent per order, one caller key, one refund job key, one restoration per reservation, one post-handoff case per verified refund, and one resolution per case.

Safe diagnosis

Operations can inspect IDs, safe states, times, job state, transition references, restoration completion, recovery, and alert state without raw payloads, credentials, or sensitive customer data.

Stable identities and business keys
  • Intent: unique order plus unique caller idempotency key.
  • Refund job: full-refund:<payment-attempt-id>:<refund-request-transition-id>.
  • Verified provider truth: provider event or protected reconciliation deduplication key.
  • Stock restoration: consumed reservation primary key.
  • Post-handoff case: unique verified-refund transition.
  • Administrative resolution: unique case and resolving transition.

10

What implementation must prove

RPRT-46 owns ordered migrations, protected predicates and commands, grants, pgTAP tests, concurrency tests, and generated types. These checks define completion.

One request creates one immutable intent, one payment transition, one audit result, and one refund job.
Duplicate requests and provider events create no duplicate effect or customer email.
Intent blocks all six forward commands and cannot be changed or withdrawn.
Intent-first, label-first, carrier-first, pickup-first, and local-handoff-first races are deterministic.
Concurrent refund and handoff paths use the common lock order without deadlock or mixed truth.
Verified pre-handoff refund applies every local effect atomically and rolls all back on failure.
Every consumed reservation stays consumed; complete quantity restores all-or-none and at most once.
Restoration works from unfulfilled, in production, and ready; assisted orders have no stock effect.
Duplicate and cancelled-order refunds cannot satisfy accepted-settlement intent.
Post-handoff refund preserves delivery truth, permits tracking, opens one case, and alerts independently.
Verified provider cancellation maps to failed; unverified evidence cannot release stock.
Late payment never reopens a cancelled order; historical settlement grants no unsafe authority.
Intent constraints prove fixed kind, required actor and reason, pointer equality, and exact transition binding.
Restoration constraints prove same order, same product, exact quantity, and complete eligible set.
Post-handoff resolution proves one guarded outcome and no lifecycle, stock, refund, or email effects.
Cross-order references, direct mutation, and unauthorized command execution fail.
