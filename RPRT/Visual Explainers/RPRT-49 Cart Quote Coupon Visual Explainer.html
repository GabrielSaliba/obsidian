Implementation walkthrough / RPRT-49

Cart intent enters. Commercial truth does not.

A PostgreSQL boundary for one persistent account cart, exact one-use shipping evidence, and globally limited merchandise coupons. The browser can request changes, but it cannot author prices, shipping, discounts, totals, or consumption state.

TL;DR

Authenticated users get one non-expiring cart. Guests keep carts locally until a trusted server records an opaque quote. Quotes bind exact cart, current package data, destination, parcel, provider, amount, and a finite validity of at most twenty-four hours. Coupon usage and quote consumption commit together or not at all.

01

Guest checkout: from local intent to protected evidence

The quote is one shipping-choice record, not a guest cart, order, contact record, or raw token.

B
After validation

PostgreSQL stores a cart hash, package, parcel, destination, service, amount, provider reference, and expiry evidence. The guest gets only the opaque quote ID.

C
At order creation

The verified guest session presents the ID. The database locks and compares exact current evidence, then consumes it once. Changed, expired, mismatched, or reused evidence fails.

03

Cart decisions that prevent hidden lock-in

Authenticated carts do not expire

One cart persists until Auth-user deletion. Timestamps support interface freshness and diagnostics; they do not silently delete customer intent.

Cart delete cascades

cart_items.cart_id on delete cascade removes transient lines when the account cart is deleted. Short-lived authenticated quotes also cascade, so they cannot block account deletion.

Product delete restricts

cart_items.product_id on delete restrict prevents physical deletion of a referenced product. Archive, status, sales-mode, and stock changes are normal updates and do not trigger this foreign key.

Stale lines stay visible

If a product becomes archived, unavailable, assisted, or out of stock, the line remains. It cannot enter a valid quote or order, but the owner can still see and remove it.

No permanent sales-mode lock

A cart line reserves no stock and stores no price. The line alone does not block a direct-to-assisted change. Later reservation and issued-order facts can block unsafe transitions.

Removal checks ownership only

remove_cart_item does not require the product to remain eligible. This is the escape path for every stale line and it has no stock effect.

New writes recheck eligibility

Add and quantity-change commands require active, published, direct-stocked products with enough current stock. Future order creation must perform the same authority check again.

04

Four tables, separate responsibilities

owner RLS

carts

One UUID per authenticated user. Persistent, non-expiring, owner-readable, command-written.

intent only

cart_items

Product plus positive quantity. No price, totals, discount, shipping, or stock reservation.

no direct reads

shipping_quotes

Immutable one-use evidence with strict package and parcel schemas, SHA-256 hashes, destination, amount, provider, and expiry.

admin read only

coupons

Merchandise-only fixed or percentage rules, minimum subtotal, validity window, optional global limit, and atomic use count.
