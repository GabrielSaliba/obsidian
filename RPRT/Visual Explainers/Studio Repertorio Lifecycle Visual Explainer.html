01

Where the two sales modes converge

They enter differently, but after placed they use the same order, payment-attempt, and fulfillment contracts.

Storefront

  • Starts from cart and checkout intent.
  • create_order creates placed.
  • Direct-stocked inventory is reserved.
→

Assisted

  • Starts from a staff-owned draft.
  • issue_assisted_order creates placed.
  • No stock reservation branch exists.

Shared after placement

Commercial snapshots are immutable. Both modes use the same payment-attempt lifecycle, settlement rule, and provider-independent fulfillment lifecycle. Delivery method and direct-stock inventory remain controlled branches, not separate order models.
